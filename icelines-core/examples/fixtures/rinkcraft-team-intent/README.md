# Prospective team-intent fixtures

Authored draft/episode/result 0.11, fictional manual scores of 10, seed 20261115.
Character rules remain shot-encounter.0.1. Target 1 deliberately guarantees neutral
branch traversal; these are accounting tests, not hockey completion-rate estimates.

The A-side dump retrieval has a local six-player D1/F3/F2/F1/D2/G legend, independent
of roster position and stable class. D1 initially holds. An active trigger and a
forecast cover arrival within a declared deadline permit D1's authored chase.
Known late cover aborts that chase; unknown trigger/cover stops unresolved.

- timely-cover: forecast arrival 1 meets deadline 1; D1 chases and collects.
- late-cover: forecast arrival 4 misses deadline 1; D1 holds and B can recover.
- unknown-cover / unknown-trigger: stop before a chase, cost or die is invented.
- inactive-trigger: no cover requirement; D1 holds.
- pressured-outlet: F2 occupies its station with an opponent nearby.
- missing-outlet: F2 is outside its declared station reach.
- reverse-direction: mirror the fixed frame and preserve timing/permission.

Cover is forecast only. `executed_cover_confirmed` remains false, and the covering
actor remains at its declared holding snapshot: no executed movement, handoff,
coverage success or counterattack shield is inferred. Actual covering motion and
its consequences are a subsequent milestone. Outlet occupancy/proximity is not a
pass lane or completion bonus. Aborted chase can still collect within hold reach.

Static validation covers complete local legends, side/domain compatibility,
references/rates/stations and explicit version opt-in, including unreached branches.
Tests retain goalies, unknown/unavailable participants, unchanged grades/dice,
local-label independence, old protocols and exact replay. Dated custody is PUCK's
`scenarios/nhl/2026-27/rinkcraft-team-intent/`.
