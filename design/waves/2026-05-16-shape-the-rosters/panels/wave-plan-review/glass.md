# GLASS Review - Shape the Rosters

## Findings

- Users need an at-a-glance compliance state: legal, missing slots, over cap, or
  unknown players.
- The recovery path should be short and command-copyable: show the exact
  `fantasy roster-shape` or `fantasy validate` command.
- Dashboard/TUI should hand off or render read-only validation before any
  browser mutation is introduced.
