A hockey character model

Attributes → skills → methods → observed results. The sheet should distinguish what a player can do, the specific tasks involved, how they attempt them, and the evidence of what happened.

The six numbers in the current preview are raw statistical signals. They belong in a Measured Record box on the right. The left column should hold capability assessments.

Goalies need their own candidate set: Tracking · Positioning · Movement · Rebounds · Recovery · Puck Play. Goalie and skater values would use different assessments and reference groups.

Scale: design for 1–20, with space for a future explicitly defined exceptional tier above 20. Each domain needs scoring anchors, a reference population and suitable evidence. Unknown remains unrated.

The existing 1–5 bands describe measured activity and outcomes relative to peers. Multiplying them by four would not produce capability assessments. The small capsule under a future attribute should show evidence status, rather than an invented game bonus.

Where the statistics belong

Measured Record: actual counts, rates, percentages, peer percentiles and source window. Those support the assessments; they are neither ability grades nor hit points.

The extra biography page suggests an extended dossier. The spell-list page suggests a longer method repertoire. Both stay outside the compact front sheet.

The hockey mappings and attribute names are design proposals. Capability rubrics, numerical grades and any 20+ tier remain to be defined.
