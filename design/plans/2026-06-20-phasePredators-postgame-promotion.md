# Phase Predators - Postgame promotion gate

> Phase Predators decides whether WP-009 postgame review and adjustment Web/API
> first-route evidence can become bounded prepared-cache postgame report claims,
> or whether they remain first-route evidence only.

**Created:** 2026-06-20
**Status:** Active - pulse 02 copy gate passed

---

## Frame

Phase Kraken promoted only practice focus. The active surface matrix still keeps
postgame review and postgame adjustments partial: the routes prove
prepared-cache Web/API behavior, but not causal blame assignment, automatic
correction plans, or a full postgame workflow.

Phase Predators audits that boundary and only strengthens the claim if product
copy and workflow evidence remain precise.

---

## Goals

| # | Goal | Why it matters | Acceptance signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Predators Goal 1 - Postgame inventory** | Evidence is split across WP-009 tests, route docs, and the surface matrix. | A wave inventory names both route pairs, cache keys, ViewModel, tests, and blockers. |
| 2 | **Predators Goal 2 - Product-copy gate** | Postgame copy must not imply causal blame, automatic corrections, prediction certainty, or live recomputation. | Accepted or deferred wording is recorded with explicit non-claims. |
| 3 | **Predators Goal 3 - Workflow evidence gate** | Postgame report claims need more than one rendered cache record. | Focused evidence proves ready/unavailable states, no recomputation, and no cache creation on missing reads. |
| 4 | **Predators Goal 4 - Surface matrix closeout** | The matrix must distinguish bounded report behavior from a finished postgame workflow. | `design/specs/surface-parity.md` carries exact final wording. |

---

## Non-goals

- Do not add new analytics formulas or live recomputation.
- Do not claim causal blame assignment, automatic correction plans, coaching
  authority, deployment, roster, matchup, betting, injury, transaction advice,
  or prediction certainty.
- Do not promote agent families in this phase.
