# Phase Kraken - Practice focus promotion gate

> Phase Kraken decides whether WP-009 practice-focus Web/API first-route
> evidence can become a bounded prepared-cache practice focus report claim, or
> whether it remains first-route evidence only.

**Created:** 2026-06-20
**Status:** Active - pulse 02 copy gate passed

---

## Frame

Phase Avalanche promoted only goalie readiness. The active surface matrix still
keeps practice focus partial: `/practice/focus` and
`/api/v1/practice/focus` prove prepared-cache Web/API route behavior, but not
mandatory drill plans, autonomous practice prescriptions, or a broader coaching
workflow.

Phase Kraken audits that boundary and only strengthens the claim if product
copy and workflow evidence remain precise.

## Goals

| # | Goal | Why it matters | Acceptance signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kraken Goal 1 - Practice-focus inventory** | Evidence is split across WP-009 tests, route docs, and the surface matrix. | A wave inventory names the route pair, cache key, ViewModel, tests, and blockers. |

## Non-goals

- Do not add new analytics formulas or live recomputation.
- Do not claim mandatory drill plans, autonomous practice prescriptions,
  coaching authority, deployment, roster, matchup, betting, injury, transaction
  advice, or prediction certainty.
- Do not promote postgame or agent families in this phase.
