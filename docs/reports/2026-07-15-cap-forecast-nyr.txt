DISCLOSURES
- 2026-27 uses the official $104M upper limit; 2027-28 uses the announced $113.5M projection, which remains subject to adjustment.
- Later cap limits compound the selected scenario growth rate and are not NHL/NHLPA forecasts.
- Confirmed cap hits are carried through a known expiry year; all other values are role-market estimates tied to that season's cap.
- Low/high modeled values are an 85%-115% scenario band around the role midpoint.
- Team totals use a deterministic 23-player active-roster scenario: up to 14 forwards, 7 defensemen, and 2 goalies, ranked by games played and production.
- Modeled market values apply a 5% age discount at 30-31 and a 15% discount at 32 or older.
- Player production and role are held at the source-season level while age advances in each forecast season.
NON-CLAIMS
- This report does not predict trades, waivers, buyouts, retention, injuries, roster turnover, term, clauses, or signing bonuses.
- Current-roster market cost is not the same as a team's legally committed cap payroll.
- Missing or low-sample production is classified conservatively and never represented as a confirmed salary.
