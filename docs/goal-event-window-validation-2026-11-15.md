encounter with the fixed goal plane whose linear2D intersection lies within a
regulation-derived six-foot opening. Results retain integer sample brackets,
linear fractions, intersection and an up-to-ten-frame earlier comparison.
Unknown reference/scale/direction stays unavailable; missing samples are not
bridged. Touching the plane then retreating is not a forward encounter beyond it.

accuracy are unverified. No candidate is selected as the goal and the raw
audit's exact-goal field remains unchanged. Repeated later encounters are retained.
Absent candidates do not dispute an official goal; closest-sample distance and
forward encounters outside the opening remain diagnostic fields. Criteria were
not widened after inspecting exceptions.

The study adapter recomputes ordered credited-actor nearest-body candidates on
each prefix ending at the pre-crossing sample. It does not filter a previously
truncated example list. Goalie snapshots are explicitly reference comparisons,
not release/response timing or positioning grades. Native/API/WASM expansion is
not claimed; this increment's first consumer is the generated study packet.
